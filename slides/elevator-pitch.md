# Roberto Elevator Pitch (Easy English)

## Core Framing Line
Roberto is user-centered at the front door and rescuer-centered in operations. Citizens are the signal; rescuers are the response engine — Roberto connects both in one real-time workflow.

## Self Intro Line
Hi, I'm **Xy**. I am a full-stack developer at **HeyApril**, a US startup building a tax SaaS optimizer for creators. I previously worked as an AI Engineer Intern at **AIFirst** and as an AI QA Engineer at **Boost Capital**, a Singapore startup.

## 20-Second Version
Hi, I am building **Roberto**. It is a flood response platform for Metro Manila. It combines flood hazard data, project coverage data, and live incident reporting, so communities can act faster and safer during floods.

## 40-Second Version
Roberto helps people before and during floods. Before floods, it shows which areas are high-risk and which areas are still under-protected. During floods, citizens can send location pings, coordinators verify and assign, and responders get clear incident context. It also gives local guidance with weather, hazard level, and nearest critical facilities.

## 60-Second Version (Pitch to Judges)
Roberto is climate adaptation technology for Metro Manila. It combines NOAH hazard zones, DPWH flood-control project coverage, and OCHA city boundaries into one operational map. In incident mode, reports move through a human workflow: Ping, Verified, Prioritized, Assigned, Resolved. For each location, Roberto provides hazard-aware guidance, nearest facilities, and route context. AI assists with local recommendations, but humans remain accountable for decisions. Our goal is simple: help communities understand flood behavior, coordinate faster, and reduce harm.

## Coverage Score Defense (Verbatim)

1. What it is: "Coverage score is an infrastructure readiness index, not a flood prediction score."
2. How it works: "It combines spatial protection overlap (raw_coverage_ratio) with project completion (avg_progress) into effective_coverage_score."
3. Why it matters: "It tells us where hazard is high but practical protection is still weak, so coordinators can pre-position teams and prioritize vulnerable areas first."
