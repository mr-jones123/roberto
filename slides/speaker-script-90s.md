# Roberto 90-Second Script

Use this framing line early: Roberto is user-centered at the front door and rescuer-centered in operations. Citizens are the signal; rescuers are the response engine — Roberto connects both in one real-time workflow.

## 0:00-0:12
Hi, I'm **Xy**. I am a full-stack developer at **HeyApril**, a US startup building a tax SaaS optimizer for creators. I previously worked as an AI Engineer Intern at **AIFirst** and as an AI QA Engineer at **Boost Capital**, a Singapore startup.

## 0:12-0:25
The problem is simple: people need help fast, but flood information is often too broad and too late.

## 0:25-0:45
Roberto combines three open datasets: **NOAH flood hazard**, **DPWH flood-control projects**, and **OCHA city boundaries**. This shows where risk is high, where protection exists, and where gaps remain.

## 0:45-1:05
In incident mode, citizens send a location-tagged ping. Then the team moves it through a human workflow: **Ping, Verified, Prioritized, Assigned, Resolved**.

## 1:05-1:20
For each ping, Roberto adds local guidance: hazard level, live weather, nearest critical facilities, and route ETA. AI helps, but people make final decisions.

## 1:20-1:30
Our answer is this: technology should not fight nature. It should help communities understand water patterns, adapt earlier, and protect each other.
